Our story

Ember & Bean started as a weekend coffee cart run by three friends who couldn't find a cup they loved close to home. A few hundred early mornings later, the cart became a café on Roast Street.

We still roast in small batches every week, and we still know most of our regulars by name. The coffee has got better; the welcome hasn't changed.

What we care about
